FAQ

Questions people ask us.

Can't find yours? Write to ola@destaca.site.

How long until my site is online?

A presentation website is ready two to three weeks after we receive your photos and business details. We write the copy.

How long until I show up on Google?

The page is indexed within days. Climbing the rankings usually takes three to six months, depending on competition. Anyone promising first place in a week is selling something else.

Do I own the domain and the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and you have access to everything. If you ever want to switch agency, we hand over the files at no cost.

Do I have to pay a monthly fee?

No. The first year of domain and hosting is included. After that, renewal starts at 60 € per year. Monthly SEO is optional and has no lock-in.

Is the free analysis really free?

Yes. Type your website address and see the result instantly, with no sign-up. We do not store the addresses you analyse.

Do you work with companies outside Portugal?

Yes. We build websites in English, Portuguese and Spanish and do SEO for several countries. Meetings are online.
