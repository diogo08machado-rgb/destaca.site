FAQ

Questions people ask us.

Can't find yours? Write to contacto@destaca.site.

How much does a website for my business cost?

A presentation website costs 490 € (fixed price, excluding VAT), with custom design, copy, a mobile version, basic SEO and hosting for year one. If you already have a site, the Site upgrade costs 290 €. A site with several pages, a blog and several languages costs from 1090 €. The domain is paid separately, each year.

How long until my site is online?

A presentation website is ready two to three weeks after we receive your photos and business details. We write the copy.

How long until I show up on Google?

The page is indexed within days. Climbing the rankings usually takes three to six months, depending on competition. Anyone promising first place in a week is selling something else.

Do I own the domain and the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and you have access to everything. If you ever want to switch agency, we hand over the files at no cost.

Do I have to pay a monthly fee?

No. The first year of hosting is included and, after that, renewal is yearly. The domain is paid separately, also each year. Monthly SEO is optional and has no lock-in.

Is the free analysis really free?

Yes. Type your website address and see the result instantly, with no sign-up. We do not store the addresses you analyse.

Do you work with companies outside Portugal?

Only within Europe. We work with businesses in Portugal and other European countries, in English, Portuguese and Spanish, with online meetings. We do not build websites for businesses outside Europe.

Do you do SEO for ChatGPT and other AI search?

Yes. We write clear answers to your customers' questions, add structured data and keep your business details consistent everywhere, so ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI answers can understand and cite your business. It is included in the Monthly SEO for Professionals plan.
